# ADR-004: Audit Is an Independently Deployed Service

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 24 November 2026

## Context
Audit records are generated by multiple business services and need a clear ownership and protection boundary.

## Decision
Audit is an independently deployed service with its own database. It owns `AUDIT_LOG`.

## Consequences
- Business services submit audit events or requests to Audit.
- Business services do not insert directly into the Audit database.
- Audit ingestion, retry, durability and failure behaviour must be defined.
- Audit access is restricted to authorized users.
